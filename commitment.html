Quality Commitment

The rationale for creating High Cans Corporation was to enhance the quality, integrity and scope of services available to industrial scrap generators and consumers. We share a commitment to performance, customer satisfaction and environmental responsibility; we are ISO 9001 and ISO 9002 certified holder for exceptional process control and product quality.

About HCC

High Cans Corporation Sdn Bhd is a consolidated scrap metal dealer and a metal recycler, operating both yard and warehouse operations from its 50,000 sq feet facility in Sungai Besi, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Our facility is one of the largest in the district.
