About Us

Philosophy

Vision

High Cans Corporation to continue to develop new and enhanced services locally & internationally, and to meet the changing needs of its clients & the marketplace. We envisage increasing our current turnover from USD 100 Million to USD 200 Million in 3 years.

Mission

The mission of High Cans Corporation is to help redefine the Metal Recycling Industry to meet today's challenges and tomorrow's opportunities by being the industry leader in safety and environmental compliance, by building lasting customer relationships, by fostering professional growth for employees, and by sharing our success with the community. We are an ISO 9001 certified holder.

About HCC

High Cans Corporation Sdn Bhd is a consolidated scrap metal dealer and a metal recycler, operating both yard and warehouse operations from its 50,000 sq feet facility in Sungai Besi, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
